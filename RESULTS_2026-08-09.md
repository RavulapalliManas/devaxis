# devaxis results — the foreclosure program

**Date:** 2026-08-09 · **Platform:** 1× trn1.32xlarge (32 NeuronCores), certified Q0/Q2/Q3 ·
**Scale:** ~890 terminal training runs (798 recounted from files at 03:47 UTC; k3tau, chi8k,
and the k2exp2 tranche completed since; the K4X expansion is in flight) · **Model:** 4.9M-param
GPT (6L, d256), K entity→obligation bindings, closed vocab.

Every number below traces to a run JSON on the box and a committed script; the script is named
next to each result. Nothing is carried forward from notes or memory. Statistics marked
**registered** were declared before the data; statistics marked *post-hoc* were not.

**The task in one paragraph.** Each sequence presents K bindings, then a RECAP block (always
supervised — the "read" circuit) and one queried answer token (supervised with probability p —
the "use" circuit). Data is a pure function of (stream, step), so two runs with different p
schedules see identical bytes and differ only in the loss mask. Checkpoint forks replay
bitwise (gate Q2). Emergence criterion everywhere: query accuracy ≥ chance + 0.5·(1−chance),
read from each run's own recorded chance level.

---

## 1. Same behavior, different futures — confirmed (the flagship)

Two K=2 models sit at chance on the query task with recap = 1.000, on identical byte streams,
and then receive identical full supervision. One never formed the circuit (supply was cut
before formation); the other formed it and lost it (supply was cut after formation, same 10k
gap). Their futures differ:

| n = 40 parents, 10k gap, 60k follow-up both arms | reacquired | KM median (rescued steps) |
|---|---|---|
| never-formed | **31/40** | 12,500 |
| formed-then-collapsed | **18/40** | not reached by 60,000 |

**Registered paired McNemar: 17 vs 4 discordant, p = 0.0072** (`analysis/mcnemar_k2.py`).
**Corrected primary — KM/log-rank on the rescued clock: χ² = 5.96, p = 0.0146**
(`analysis/km_rescued.py`).

The full look history, because it matters: n=8 exploratory (8/8 vs 4/8, p≈0.077) → n=24
(McNemar 0.057, but the formed arm had half the follow-up; the corrected analysis read
p = 0.216 — the near-claim was a censoring artifact) → n=40 with follow-up matched by
construction: confirmed on both tests.

Two things this establishes. First: current behavior plus the read circuit's state do not
determine a model's future — a hidden state does. Second, the direction: **the absorbing state
is formed-then-collapsed, not never-formed.** Having once mastered the task and lost it is
worse than never having learned it, at matched load, gap, age-profile, and data.

At K=4 the same construction exists in the original arms (tau10k vs postresc are matched on
age, cumulative supervised count, and stream, per parent): there **both** routes freeze
(0/20 never-formed, 0/7 formed-collapsed). The dissociation lives at K=2; at K=4 the two
routes converge into one absorbing state.

## 2. The foreclosure clock — plasticity dies before behavior moves

Eight K=4 models were forked at step 4,000 and deprived (p=0) with checkpoints every 1,000
steps. At each age, a pulse of full supervision was applied and its effect read on the logit
channel (Δgold_mass, pulse minus same-bytes trajectory control). `analysis/chi_matrix.py`:

| deprivation age | χ, δ=4000 pulse | χ, δ=8000 pulse |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (positive control) | **+0.164 ± 0.172** | **+0.513 ± 0.301** |
| 2,000 | +0.051 ± 0.088 | — |
| 4,000 | +0.087 ± 0.120 | — |
| 6,000 | +0.022 ± 0.035 | — |
| 8,000 | +0.006 ± 0.014 | +0.003 ± 0.012 |
| 16,000 | +0.008 ± 0.016 | +0.006 ± 0.017 |
| 20,000 | +0.006 ± 0.020 | — |

Query accuracy sits at chance in pulse and control alike the whole time, and recap holds
1.000: **the capacity to respond to supervision decays ~30–100× within the first ~8k steps of
deprivation, before any behavioral evidence exists.** The age-0 row is the built-in positive
control (those forks are the parent state, known plastic). The decay timescale matches the
τ dose-response knee (§3).

Per-parent paired decline (χ at age 0 vs mean χ at ≥8k): **7/8 positive at both pulse widths,
with different dissenting parents** (s3 at δ=4000 — its 4k pulse under-probes a slow emerger;
s1 at δ=8000). Registered sign test p = 0.070, pinned by n=8 granularity; *post-hoc* Wilcoxon
signed-rank p = 0.016. The K4X expansion (8 more parents, declared rule: primary δ=8000,
≥13/16 → p = 0.021) is running.

**The constructive mirror image** (`analysis/chi_normal.py`): the same pulse probe applied
along *normal* (supervised) development shows plasticity **growing** toward emergence — δ=1k
response +0.000 at ages 1–2k, +0.012 at 4k, **+0.235 ± 0.232 at 8k** (late-age cells n=2–3,
selected slow emergers). Supervision grows the very plasticity that deprivation destroys, over
the same interval. Plasticity is supply-dependent in both directions.

Instrument notes, for honesty: δ=1000 is at the noise floor even on plastic states; δ=250
recorded no post-fork logit and is excluded; the 2k/4k inversion in the δ=4000 column is
within seed noise.

## 3. The τ_c(K) phase boundary — foreclosure is load-dependent

Fork every baseline pre-emergence, cut supply for τ steps, restore fully, count emergence:

| load | fork | τ=2k | τ=5k | τ=10k | τ=20k |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K=2 (fork@1,000) | | 8/8 | 8/8 | **8/8** | — |
| K=3 (fork@3,000) | | 8/8 | 7/8 | **0/8** | — |
| K=4 (fork@4,000) | | 4/8 | 2/8 | **0/20** | 0/16 |

At K=2, deprivation only *delays* (emergence up to ~36k rescued steps late). At K=3 and K=4 it
forecloses, at load-dependent doses: **τ_c(2) > 10k, τ_c(3) ∈ (5k, 10k], τ_c(4) ≈ 2–5k.**
K=3 τ=10k (0/8) vs K=2 τ=10k (8/8): Fisher p ≈ 1.6e-4. K=4 dose trend: Cochran-Armitage
p = 7.8e-05. One K=3 τ=2k run crossed the criterion at 24,250 and slipped back to 0.648 by
budget — counted as emerged under the pre-registered first-crossing rule, flagged as an
emergence-instability observation.

Supporting structure at K=4: foreclosure operates at *every* pre-emergence age (window
experiment, 22 runs; lower-edge test, 24 runs — no edge in either direction), which is why
the honest name is supply-dependent foreclosure, not a critical period.

**The emergence ladder** (baselines, within a 120k budget): K=2 — 8/8, median ~3,100 steps;
K=3 — 8/8, 6,500–13,000; K=4 — 7/8, ~7,000–31,750; K=8 — **2/8** (48,750 and 72,000; six runs
flat at chance through the full 120k). The **read circuit saturates at step 250 at every K**
— the read/use gap at K=8 spans 48,500 steps with recap pinned at 1.000. (K=6 was dropped on
platform grounds: neuronx-cc fails deterministically on that shape — fast SIGHUP even as a
solo compile; not a scientific result.)

## 4. Collapse and maintenance economics

- **Collapse latency is load-independent.** A formed circuit under p=0 falls to chance in
  750–1,750 steps at K=4 (7/7) and 1,000–2,000 at K=2 (16/16 across both gap arms), while
  recap never moves.
- **Recoverability is load-dependent.** After a 3k gap: K=2 recovers 7/8; K=4 recovers 0/7
  (Fisher p ≈ 0.0014). After a 10k gap at K=2: 18/40 (the flagship's formed arm).
- **The block is in the weights.** Optimizer-state reset 0/7, LR re-warm 0/7, both 0/7,
  weight-decay-zero replication 7/7 collapse with 0/16 rescue — every non-weight mechanism
  is excluded.
- **Maintenance is cheap but load-priced.** Fraction of runs ending ≥0.75 while holding at
  dose p: K=4 — 0.01: 1/7, 0.02: 3/7, 0.03: 7/7 (two recovered *from chance* under continuous
  3%), 0.04–0.20: 7/7. K=2 — 0.005: 1/8, 0.01: 4/8, 0.03: 7/8. Keeping a capability alive
  costs a few percent of the supervision that created it, and half the load roughly halves
  the price.

## 5. What did not matter (the nulls, equally load-bearing)

- **Repetition structure**: recurrence type × spacing × order factorial (49 runs) — all
  p_holm = 1.0. How exposure is distributed moved nothing; when supply arrives decided
  everything.
- **Tiny doses do not rescue formation**: p ∈ {0.001, 0.005, 0.01} in the deprivation window
  then full rescue — 2/8, 1/8, 0/8 (pooled 3/24 vs p=0's 0/20, Fisher p = 0.24, ns).
- **Dose alone and max-gap alone both fail** (continuity experiment, 32 runs at identical
  dose 300): continuous-3% produced 1 emergence + 3 censored climbers at 0.477–0.496; all
  24 burst runs — max-gap 970 and 9,700 alike — ended ≤ 0.271 (*post-hoc* rank test
  p ≈ 0.039). A 970-step gap already behaves like a 9,700-step gap; the gap that matters is
  on the order of the ~1k collapse latency. The parametric dose/gap/continuity model is
  quasi-separated at this n — the verdict is the table, not a fit.

## 6. Corrections kept on the record

1. A claimed 17× throughput gain — retracted after an A/A test measured 0.99 (compile-cache
   warming).
2. "Mastery protects plasticity" — retracted; it came from a gap-unmatched comparison, and
   the matched design reversed it.
3. The n=24 flagship near-claim (p=0.057) — voided by my own censoring correction (p=0.216),
   then settled properly at n=40.
4. Seven K=8 baselines lost ~58k steps to a restart that swept the unit cgroup — re-derived
   deterministically (keyed streams; Q2); `KillMode=process` now makes the class impossible.
5. Eight jobs silently lost to a builder core-assignment race — recovered; the dispatcher now
   locks cores at launch time.
6. K=6 excluded for compiler failure, recorded as a platform limit, not a finding.
